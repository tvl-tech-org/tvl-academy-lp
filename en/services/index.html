How we work

Every engagement starts from your processes, not a template. We look at where AI already touches the work, where the value is, and where the risk sits, then we scope the smallest useful step. You keep control throughout: our compliance and configuration work is advisory, so your administrator applies the settings and we never ask for system access or credentials.

What you leave with is meant to last. That means a decision log, an AI registry, a 90-day roadmap, and per-role assistants, not a slide deck. The trainer and advisor is Alexandru Dan, CEO of TVL Tech and a Generative AI lecturer at ASE Bucharest, so the work is grounded in real delivery and current practice.

Frequently asked questions

What does TVL's AI consultancy cover?

Three things: a practical AI strategy and adoption plan, EU AI Act compliance and governance, and training that gets your teams using AI correctly. Each is built on your real processes.

Do you help with EU AI Act compliance?

Yes. We map your AI use against the Act, classify risk, put the controls and an AI registry in place, cover the Article 4 AI literacy obligation, and leave you with audit-ready documentation.

Is this consulting or training?

Both. We advise on strategy and compliance, and we train your teams to run it. Most engagements combine the two, because a plan nobody can execute does not help.

Who is it for?

Mid-market and corporate teams in Romania and the EU that use AI at work and need to do it correctly, safely, and in line with the EU AI Act.

How do we start?

A 30-minute call to see where you are and where the value and the risk sit. From there we scope a strategy, a compliance engagement, training, or a combination.

Not sure where to start with AI?

Book 30 minutes with Alexandru Dan. We will look at where AI can help and where the EU AI Act applies, and agree the smallest useful first step.
