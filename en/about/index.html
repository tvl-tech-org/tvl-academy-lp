About TVL Tech

TVL Tech is a Romanian AI company that helps organizations adopt AI correctly. We build automation, advise on AI strategy and EU AI Act compliance, and run the TVL Academy training program, all on your real processes rather than a generic playbook.

What we do

Companies do not struggle to hear about AI. They struggle to turn it into everyday work that is correct, safe, and worth the effort. TVL closes that distance, from a first prompt library to an AI strategy a leadership team can act on.

How we work

Two principles hold across everything we do. First, we start from your real processes, not a template, so the output fits the way your teams already work. Second, our consulting is advisory: we tell you what to configure, document, and control, and your own administrator applies it. We never ask for system access or credentials, so you keep control of your systems and your data.

What you leave with is meant to last: a prompt library, per-role assistants, an automation plan, an AI registry, and documentation you can show to a customer, an auditor, or a regulator.

Who we work with

We work with companies and public institutions in Romania and across Europe that want to adopt AI without creating risk, including Romstal, RMT Labs, Arcanna, and Ocean Enterprise. Programs are delivered in Romanian or English, on-site anywhere in Romania or online.
