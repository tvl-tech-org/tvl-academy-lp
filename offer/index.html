Workstream 1

Claude.ai organisational configuration advisory

Consultancy is billed at €100 per actual hour. A typical guidance and review engagement is estimated at 5 to 10 hours, or €500-€1,000.

Guidance and review scope

  • Discuss organisational goals, security requirements and current working practices.
  • Compare Claude Team and Enterprise options and recommend a suitable starting point.
  • Explain seat, billing, owner, administrator and user access choices.
  • Review domain, single sign-on and user provisioning options with the client's IT team, where relevant.
  • Walk through the available data, feedback, retention, sharing, public project and connector checkboxes.
  • Recommend a structure for shared Projects, company rules, brand guidance and curated knowledge.
  • Discuss the organisation Skills policy and an initial approved Skill catalogue.
  • Review possible MCP connections, permission boundaries and rollout options.
  • Provide a decision log and step-by-step checklist for the client's administrator.

Boundaries and prerequisites

  • The client appoints an authorised administrator and the relevant IT, security or data protection contacts.
  • The client's administrator selects and applies every setting in the organisation's own account.
  • TVL Academy provides guidance and checklist validation, without requesting direct system access.
  • TVL Academy does not receive passwords, authentication tokens or administrator credentials.
  • Custom integrations, remote Model Context Protocol connector development and data migration are outside this advisory scope.
  • Legal assessment, data protection impact assessment and penetration testing are not included.
  • Additional configuration guidance and advisory are billed at €100 per hour with prior approval.

Baseline recommendation for 10 to 20 managers

Start with Claude Team Standard seats, subject to the client's security review. Team includes central administration, single sign-on and organisation-level controls. Enterprise becomes relevant when the organisation requires controls such as SCIM provisioning, audit logs, custom data retention or network-level restrictions.

Workstream 2

Custom executive AI programme

Three practical modules for 10 to 20 managers. Each module is adapted to the organisation's sector, priorities, tools and real management decisions.

01

AI Awareness

TVL Academy's three-hour awareness course, adapted to the organisation and its management team.

Understand AI

Learn what artificial intelligence, generative AI and language models are, supported by the key moments in AI history.

See Generative AI in Action

Explore practical demonstrations using text, images, documents and information from different sources.

Prompt Clearly

Turn a real need into a clear request with the right context, expected output and useful constraints.

Apply AI to Real Work

Identify useful applications for daily management tasks, departments and the organisation's own priorities.

Recognise Errors and Hallucinations

Detect unsupported claims, invented sources and overconfident answers before relying on an AI output.

Use AI Safely

Protect sensitive information, understand model limitations and decide when human verification is required.

Outcome: a shared AI foundation, direct practical experience and clear rules for when human verification is required.

02

Applied AI Literacy · Projects, Skills, Agents and Claude Code

Participants move from individual chats to reusable AI working systems. Each manager configures practical Claude components and completes a guided first task in Claude Code.

Claude Projects

Create a focused workspace with reusable knowledge, instructions, access rules and a clear business purpose.

Reusable Skills

Focused Agents

Build a Small Internal Tool

Verify and Approve

03

AI Strategy, Governance and Human-Agent Operating Model

Participants design the company's AI operating system, covering Claude configuration decisions, reusable company capabilities, governed human-agent delivery, regulatory readiness and investment priorities.

Company AI Foundation

Create organisation-wide Skills, company rules, brand standards, shared Claude Projects and a curated knowledge base.

Identity and Data Controls

Define account access levels, sharing and retention choices, approved data spaces and escalation paths.

MCP and Agent Architecture

Select approved connectors, permission boundaries and agent roles for secure access to company tools and knowledge.

Multi-Agent Project Board

Coordinate specialist agents and human roles across intake, build, review, approval, release and monitoring.

Human Oversight and Ethical AI

Assign accountable owners, human-in-the-loop gates, evidence requirements, verification, incident response and stop rules.

Regulatory Readiness and AI Registry

Classify use cases against the AI Act, GDPR and relevant legislation, then record purpose, data, risk, controls and status.

AI Portfolio and 90-Day Roadmap

Prioritise use cases by impact, feasibility and risk, then assign pilot criteria, owners, KPIs and next decisions.

Outcome: a draft Company AI Operating Blueprint, including target Claude configuration decisions, one mapped human-agent workflow, a starter AI Registry and an agreed 90-day action plan.
